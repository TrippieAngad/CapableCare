# CapableCare Community Guide

## App Theme

CapableCare is built around a simple idea: a healthy elder-care community works best when people show up for elders with steadiness, dignity, patience, and selflessness. The app is not only a scheduling portal. It is a coordination space where families, caretakers, and the broader care circle share responsibility, reduce confusion, and make daily support feel trustworthy rather than transactional.

The product tone should reflect service, gratitude, and human reliability. Every feature is meant to help elders feel seen, help families feel informed, and help caretakers feel equipped to provide calm, respectful care.

## Overview Tab

The Overview tab is the main confidence center. It gives a quick picture of the elder's current care situation without forcing the user to inspect every record manually.

Overview includes:
- the elder's identity and age
- the assigned caretaker or trust profile
- on-time and verified visit metrics when available
- the latest verified visit status
- upcoming visits that the family or caretaker should be aware of
- a family collaboration section showing who is involved
- a "What changed this week" summary that highlights meaningful recent shifts

The intent of Overview is to replace anxiety and guesswork with calm situational awareness.

## Client Tab

The Client tab is the care profile and plan surface used in the caretaker workflow. It centralizes the elder's core needs and preferences so support remains consistent even across time and across people.

Client includes:
- emergency contact information
- subscription tier visibility
- transportation needs
- accessibility settings such as large text and high contrast
- care goals
- daily routines
- restrictions
- medication schedule

The intent of Client is continuity. A caretaker should be able to understand how to serve the elder with respect, precision, and consistency.

## Updates Tab

The Updates tab is the verified care-log workspace. It records what happened, what changed, and whether anything needs attention.

For caretakers, Updates supports:
- submitting a verified visit
- selecting visit type
- reporting visit status
- recording check-in and check-out times
- entering checklist items
- marking checklist completion
- entering wellness scores like mood, mobility, appetite, and engagement
- recording medication adherence
- flagging home safety issues
- adding visit notes
- attaching optional photo proof
- escalating incidents or emergencies

For families and caretakers, Updates also shows:
- the care updates feed
- visit notes
- checklists
- assessment summaries
- incident flags
- emergency escalation indicators

The intent of Updates is truthfulness and accountability. It documents care work clearly so the community around the elder can respond early and responsibly.

## Messages Tab

The Messages tab is the elder-care match maker and care communication area. It helps families identify a strong elder-care buddy fit and communicate about preferences.

Messages includes:
- a highlighted elder-care buddy match
- match quality indicators such as points, likability, punctuality, and reliability
- a summary of why the match fits the elder's needs
- filters for preferences such as gender, schedule, and support style
- communication context for coordination between participants

The intent of Messages is not casual chatting alone. It is relationship matching and communication in service of dependable human care.

## Tasks Tab

The Tasks tab is the operational coordination board. It turns intentions into concrete follow-up items that different people in the care circle can track.

Tasks includes:
- submitted tasks
- task title and description
- task type
- assigned owner when available
- priority
- status
- due date

The intent of Tasks is shared responsibility. Small needs should not be forgotten just because many people care.

## Plans Tab

The Plans tab is the subscription and service-plan surface for family users. It helps the family understand service levels and choose the support tier that matches the elder's needs.

Plans includes:
- available subscription plans
- monthly pricing
- visit frequency
- update frequency
- included features
- switching between plans

The intent of Plans is clarity. Families should know what support structure they are choosing and how that changes the level of care coordination.

## Support Tab

The Support tab hosts ECareAI. This is the guided assistant experience for questions about care coordination, risk summaries, visit logistics, and what changed recently.

Support includes:
- a text chat mode
- a speech mode
- grounded responses about the elder's care context
- explanations of recent updates
- help with buddy recommendations
- help with transportation coordination
- concise, trustworthy answers rather than vague generalities

The intent of Support is guided understanding. It should help the user act on real care information with confidence.

## Family Role

For family members, CapableCare acts as a confidence center. It should make them feel included, informed, and able to contribute without needing to micromanage every interaction.

The family experience centers on:
- visibility
- trust
- communication
- plan management
- clear next steps

## Caretaker Role

For caretakers, CapableCare acts as a verified workspace. It should reduce ambiguity and make it easy to deliver respectful care that aligns with the elder's routines and preferences.

The caretaker experience centers on:
- visit verification
- continuity of care
- issue escalation
- practical record keeping
- dependable coordination

## Community Philosophy

CapableCare should consistently communicate that elder support is a community effort. The strongest care systems are made of people who help without ego, notice small changes early, and act with selflessness. The platform should make that spirit visible through every tab: confidence in Overview, continuity in Client, accountability in Updates, connection in Messages, coordination in Tasks, clarity in Plans, and guidance in Support.
